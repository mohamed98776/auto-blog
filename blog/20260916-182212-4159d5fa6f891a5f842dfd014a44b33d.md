<p><span style="font-weight: 400;"><span class="lead-in">Foundation has</span> gotten a lot better at not looking like foundation. Today’s best formulas sit somewhere between skincare and makeup, using serum like textures and skin friendly ingredients to blur redness, uneven tone and texture without covering everything up.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">If you want a little more than a skin tint but hate the feeling of a full face of makeup, these are the clean foundations worth knowing now.</span></p>

<h2><span class="lead-in">The trick to making foundation look like skin</span></h2>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;"><br />
Even the best foundation can start looking like makeup when you use too much. Start with skincare and give it a few minutes to settle, then apply foundation primarily through the center of the face or wherever you actually need evening out. Blend outward and add another thin layer only over redness, discoloration or blemishes.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The goal is not perfectly uniform skin. Leaving some natural variation visible is precisely what keeps foundation from looking like foundation.</span></p>
